## The Pivot: Producing Outcomes, not Workflows

Modern SaaS wins by delivering **outcomes**, not by forcing customers into yet another tool. Instead of asking people to migrate their data and learn a new platform, the better move is to reduce friction and deliver results directly into the systems they already use.

> We're not competitor-obsessed, we're customer-obsessed. We start with what the customer needs and work backwards.

A realtor, for example, shouldn't have to adopt a whole new platform to get value — they should simply receive curated leads and market intelligence without changing their existing workflow. The catch: solutions still need human oversight to ensure accuracy and prevent AI hallucinations.
